【Install】

　「Odekake Multi」is a game that needs to be installed on both your PC and P/ECE.
 

●If the「P/ECE SDK」has not been installed on your PC yet

・Please insert the disc that came with your P/ECE into your PC's disc drive

・Start the「P/ECE SDK」installer.
　「Odekake Multi」will install automatically along with the「P/ECE SDK」.

●If the「P/ECE SDK」has already been installed

・Has your P/ECE been updated to the latest firmware?

・If not, please make sure to update your P/ECE's firmware.
　Reference the P/ECE manual for more detailed instructions.
　(Please view the「index.htm」file inside your「piece」folder in a browser.
　　Inside the「DOCUMENT」page, refer to the「System Install」instructions.)
　

●If the SDK is installed and you have updated your P/ECE to the latest firmware

・Please start the「Odekake Multi」application.
　「Windows」Button→「P/ECE」→「Odekake Multi」

・After the opening movie, (which you can left click to skip)
  A message saying (tl note: this may not be accurate as I am translating this site before the PC exe)
　「Installing「Odekake Multi」to the P/ECE
　　Ensure the P/ECE is connected to your PC.」
　will appear.
　Ensure that you do not disconnect the USB cable during this process.

・After installing successfully, the game's prologue will begin.
　After you have finished reading the prologue, you are free to start playing the P/ECE game.

・When you feel like you've hit a good stopping point with the P/ECE game, please sync your save data with the PC game.
　This will update both your diary and rivals' stats.
 

【Uninstall】

・If you want to uninstall「Odekake Multi」from your P/ECE,
　Open the「P/ECE Communicator」program (「WinIsd」) and delete it from there.
・If you want to uninstall「Odekake Multi」from your PC,
　「Control Panel」→「Programs」→「Programs and Features」
　From this menu, you can uninstall the「P/ECE SDK」just like any other application.
