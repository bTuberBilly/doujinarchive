◆ Rules of the Game　

　・Basic Rules
　　To put it bluntly, this game is reversi (Othello). If you manage to surround your opponent's discs with your own, they'll flip and change to your color.

　・Win/Loss Conditions
　　When the board is completely filled with discs (or after 99 turns have passed), the player with the highest score wins.
　　If both players have the same score, the game ends in a draw.
　　If a player runs out of time, they lose irrespective of their current score.

　・MasteryCheater Skills
　　On your turn, you can right click to use your character's MasteryCheater Skills. Each character has 6 MasteryCheater Skills.
　　MasteryCheater Skills can only be used when you have at least 1 bar of MasteryCheater Meter.
　　All MasteryCheater Skills, with very few exceptions, will drain 1 bar of MasteryCheater Meter. (TL Note: This is inaccurate. All 3 versions of Gekka no Kishi--Oni, Hime, and Plus--support this feature, but none of the characters make use of it.)
　　(TL Note: Certain MasteryCheater Skills have inaccurate in-game descriptions. These will be marked accordingly in the README.)

　・Round Timer
　　In VS Mode, you can set the round timer to 10 sec., 3 min., 5 min., and infinite.
　　Once your timer drops below 10 sec., it will immediately refill to 10 sec. after taking your turn.
　　Running out of time results in an immediate loss.
