◆ Network Match Options (WIP)

　Upon clicking the Network Match option on the Title Screen, you will be greeted by the following prompt:

　Enter your name into the textbox, make sure the first DirectPlay option is highlighted, then click "OK" to proceed.

　First, let's go over how to create a game.
　Click the "Create Game" button to proceed to the following prompt:

　Enter a room name into the text box, then click "OK" to proceed:

　In the bottom left is your IP address. Please note it down for other players so they can join your game

・Please select your positions
　　In CPU battles, you will always be Player 1; the CPU will always be Player 2
・Spectator 1/2/3
　　Clicking these will move you into a spectator's seat.
　　Each match can have up to 3 spectators.
・Who goes first?
　　Choose which player will get to take the first turn.
　　The player who goes first will be Black.
・Turn Timer
　　Select how much time each player has to take turns.
　　If a player's time runs out during a match, they lose by default.
・Game Board
　　Select whether you want to pick which board you play on or not.

　To join someone else's game, click "Search for Game" to proceed to this prompt:

　Enter the IP Address of the host game you'd like to join. You'll then be taken back to the previous prompt:

　From here, you can either highlight the room you'd like to join and click "Join" or double click on the room name to join.
